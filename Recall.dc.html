CAPTURE ARCHITECTURE
The hard part isn't the app — it's feeding it. Realistic stack, layered by effort:

1. Easy & automatic — share-sheet for links (screen 03), phone GPS for places, Apple Health / wearables for stats, bank feed for spend.

2. Breadth — a personal DNS resolver (Pi-hole / NextDNS) logs every domain network-wide. Good for "where did my attention go," but can't see inside HTTPS (which video, which post).

3. Specifics — a browser extension + OS screen-time exports fill in the actual titles. Recall ingests all three into one timeline.
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
Logging must cost <3 seconds or you'll stop. So: a single + button everywhere, one-tap frequents that learn your habits, smart autocomplete from your own history, and everything that can be automatic, is. Manual entry is the fallback, never the default.
